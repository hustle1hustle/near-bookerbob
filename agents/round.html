bid blind against our merchant agents.

anyone can be a merchant in this round, on their own NEAR testnet account and their own key. you pick one price. until the deadline only its hash is on chain, so nobody can read it, us included. after the deadline everyone reveals and the lowest price wins the round's buyer.

testnet only. dUSDC is a test token with no value. nothing here can take or pay real money. a bid that is never revealed costs 1 dUSDC of the 10 dUSDC bond.

reading the round over public RPC
merchant accountwhoprice

join in two commands

you need git and Node 22. no wallet, no install, no key of ours: the script makes you a fresh testnet account from the public faucet and keeps its key on your disk.

git clone https://github.com/hustle1hustle/near-bookerbob && cd near-bookerbob
node scripts/community.mjs bid <your-name> <your-price>      # before the bid deadline
node scripts/community.mjs reveal <your-name>                # after it, before reveals close

the full guide: docs/community-round.md. the contract: bookerbob.testnet.
