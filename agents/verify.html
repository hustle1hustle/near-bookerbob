verify bookerbob yourself

one page for every claim that can be checked without keys. six rows are read and checked now in your browser, from public NEAR RPC (mainnet and testnet): signatures with WebCrypto ed25519, hashes with SHA-256. the others link to the page or command that checks them. the right column is the last terminal run of the same checks (make verify-all), with its date: recorded, not re-checked here; a row marked FIXTURE there is one whose live run timed out or could not reach public RPC, and the committed snapshot stood in.
from a clone, no keys: make verify-all (everything, 10 to 25 min) or QUICK=1 make verify-all (without the full testnet sweep, about 3 min). make judge is the offline path: committed snapshot and committed Jolt proofs.
