Agents collude. Pricing algorithms are known to learn to hold prices up together with no message passed (Calvano et al. 2020; Fish, Gonczarowski and Shorrer 2024).
Each seller agent commits a sealed hash and sees no rival price before the deadline; linked accounts cannot both stand in a round; a bid left sealed costs part of its bond.

Agents are unaccountable. When an AI agent sets a price, nobody can later say what it decided.
Each model decision is signed inside a TEE and named in the bid's salt on chain, so each bid traces to one signed decision.
