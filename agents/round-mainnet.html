  how it works
  
    you are a seller with one room to sell. your price goes on chain as a hash, so nobody can read it before the deadline, us included. after the deadline every bid is revealed and the contract pins it. the lowest revealed price wins, and our buyer agent pays it in Circle USDC on NEAR, against a NEAR AI verdict and a referee receipt.
    
  
    the page makes an ed25519 key in this browser. its public key in hex is your NEAR account name (an implicit account). the first 10 accounts can press "get 0.07 NEAR to start": BookerBob's faucet sends 0.07 NEAR, enough to bid. after that, send it about 0.1 NEAR from any wallet or exchange; that creates the account. NEAR asks a transaction to hold more than it spends (unused gas and storage deposits come back), and bidding ends up using about 0.01 NEAR. the rest stays on your account, and "show my key" lets you take it with you.
    
  
    BookerBob's watcher approves every account that registers in this round's category and has registered with USDC, usually within a minute or two, up to 60 accounts. it holds a key on the bazaar limited to open_round and approve_merchant, with a gas allowance NEAR enforces. the permissionless lane (a 10 USDC bond, no approval) stays on testnet, so nothing of yours is locked here.
    
  
    one visit is enough. your reveal is signed in this page and time-locked with drand until the bid deadline; after it our relayer opens it and sends it, paying the gas. it cannot change your price. you can also come back after the deadline and press reveal.
    
  
    the buyer's facts are synthetic fixture facts and the buyer is our own agent; the payment is real. one bid per account. a bid never revealed bars the account from new rounds for 24 hours. prices above 0.23 USDC are refused by this page (the buyer's budget). the buyer holds enough USDC to pay any allowed price; we settle after reveals close.
