prove it on your own device

an agent picked a hotel for a buyer. this page makes a Jolt zkVM proof, here in your browser, that the pick followed the buyer's rules. then it checks the proof, and lets you try to cheat. in use, the agent's operator makes this proof and anyone checks it.

the claim

...hotel-lisbon round on the board
...dUSDC
public price ...
  1. the pick is one of the offers
  2. its price is what the contract's price code gives for the bits the buyer revealed
  3. it fits the budget (the budget of the model-choice run; the testnet purchase replayed on the swarm page asked under 450, and the pick is the same)
  4. no offer is cheaper
the public inputs

all of this is prove/public.json. prices are dUSDC base units there (6 decimals); dUSDC is a testnet demo token with no value.

what this proves, what it does not

    

      
it proves

      
    
        
  • the decision rule over the public offers. a program that reprices every offer with the contract's price function and checks the four rules above ran to the end on exactly these inputs, and returned this pick and price.
    • 
        
  • succinctly, though here a rerun is faster. the inputs are small and all public, so rerunning the rule takes less time than checking the proof. a proof matters when an input is hidden or the checker is a contract, and neither is true here yet. the verifier does not rerun the program. it checks a small proof against the trace.
    • 
      

    

    

      
it does not prove

      
    
        
  • that the offers are honest. the offers, the budget and the revealed bits are inputs. a false input gives a true proof about false inputs.
    • 
        
  • anything on chain. this proof is verified here, in your browser, and not by the NEAR contract. it is not the on-chain standing proof: that is a different program, on a different Jolt pin, with its hash registered on chain.
    • 
        
  • secrecy. this build is not zero-knowledge.
    • 
        
  • post-quantum security. the pinned Jolt commits with Dory over the BN254 curve (transcript: Keccak). that is classical security only.
    • 
      

    

  


  

    
the zkVM is Jolt, by a16z crypto and contributors. this page runs their prover and verifier, unmodified apart from the two known wasm32 fixes, compiled to WebAssembly at commit b4eb62480d76.

    
applied the known wasm32 fixes: a usize multiply that wraps on 32-bit (a16z/jolt#1913) and a smaller tracer reserve (0xAndoroid/jolt-wasm-prover, patches 0001 and 0009). neither is ours.

    
this browser prover is a separate set of crates with its own Jolt pin; the four BookerBob guests whose program hash is registered on chain stay on a16z/jolt 997c1543efd4 and are not touched by it.

    
threads: this page registers one service worker, coi-prove-sw.js, scoped to this page and its files, so the browser allows wasm threads on a host that sends no isolation headers (the coi-serviceworker pattern, our own code). it reloads the page once on a first visit. it stores nothing and sends nothing; without it the page proves on one thread.

    
source, build and measured numbers: zk/browser/README.md. exact file sizes: prove/sizes.json.
