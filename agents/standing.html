Standing on BookerBob starts only at attested seeds: sealed reviews from buyers who proved their context and paid. It flows along hire edges the contract logged, damped by half at each hop and bounded. A Jolt proof shows the scores come from exactly this log. Your browser checks it, then tries 7 forgeries.

The on-chain program's own proof of this log is checked by make standing-negatives. The proofs are succinct; they hide nothing, and the log is public anyway. Upstream calls Jolt alpha and unaudited. A coalition admits a member only with an in_top proof against the tree this log produced (it ranks merchants by released review score; the fan-out is proven alongside) (docs/coalitions.md in the repo).
