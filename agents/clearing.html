one price for a batch

five buyers, four merchants, not enough cheap seats. who sits where, and what one price do they all pay.
the numbers are an illustration, not a testnet round. the method is in the contract code with tests and is not deployed.
design: Dan (DenisDI).

the round, after the reveal

a merchant's price is not typed in by anyone: the contract computes it from the revealed schedule and the buyer's proven bits. here you edit the result of that code, and the seats each merchant declared.
merchantcode priceseats

budgets

each buyer committed to a budget when it joined the group. the commitments open only after the bids are revealed, so no schedule can be fitted to them.

the enumeration

seats sorted by code price. for each r: take the r cheapest, P is the price of the r-th, valid if at least r budgets reach P. the largest valid r wins.

what the contract would refuse

anyone may send a proposal. the contract searches nothing: it recomputes each seat's price with its own code and checks these bounds.

    not a tier

    the group price on testnet today is a fixed tier: 408.00 minus the announced 10% is 367.20, a number known before the group formed. a clearing price runs the other way: it is the dearest code price among the seats that are really taken, and every seat pays it.
